About WOTDO

Who We Are

Women Of Tomorrow Dev't Organisation (WOTDO) is dedicated to ensuring that parents understand the basic principles of child growth and development, and the profound implications these have for children's learning.

By fostering deep awareness, we help parents recognize the critical impact of mental health on caregiving and child development, creating a foundation where every child can reach their full potential.

Our Vision

Enhancing Sustainable Rural Transformation among Young Mothers, Adolescents & Youth In Communities Across Uganda.

Our Mission

To empower at-risk youth and women through collaborative programs, parenting mentorship, and spiritual nurture—ensuring a just and humane society where there is equal sharing of opportunities and resources.

Common Questions

How can I join the parenting mentorship program?
You can join by visiting our headquarters in Kigogwa or contacting us via email. We host monthly sessions for new parents and mentors.
Where are your regional projects located?
Our primary projects are currently running in Kigogwa (Kawempe), Buliisa (Climate Outreach), Terego District (Nutrition), and Bondo Village (Sanitary Pad Making).
Do you accept volunteers?
Yes! We are always looking for mentors, environmental advocates, and youth leaders. Please reach out via our contact page to apply.

Our Community Focus

We target specific demographic and systemic areas to maximize our impact in Uganda.

👥 Demographics Served

Children, Adolescents, Youth, and Women. We ensure no one is left behind in the pursuit of a better future.

🌾 Key Issue Areas

Food & Agriculture and essential Sector Services provision to support sustainable community living.

📍 Geography

Headquartered and fully operational within the communities of Uganda.
