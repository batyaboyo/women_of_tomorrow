## About

**Women of Tomorrow Development Organization (WOTDO)** is a community-based organization headquartered in **Kigogwa, Kawempe — Uganda**. We empower at-risk youth and women through collaborative programs, parenting mentorship, and spiritual nurture — working towards a just and humane society where there is equal sharing of opportunities, resources, and services.

### Vision
Enhancing Sustainable Rural Transformation among Young Mothers, Adolescents & Youth in Communities Across Uganda.

### Mission
To empower at-risk youth and women through collaborative programs, parenting mentorship, and spiritual nurture — ensuring a just and humane society.
