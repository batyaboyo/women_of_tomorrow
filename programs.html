Programs & Impact

Driving Sustainable Change

Through dedicated efforts and strategic partnerships, we address fundamental challenges in our communities to enable sustainable livelihoods.

Our initiatives are designed not just to provide immediate relief, but to build long-term resilience and empower individuals to become drivers of their own success.

Our Core Initiatives

Parenting Mentorship Program

Parents understand the basic principles of child growth and the impact of mental health on caregiving to ensure a positive learning future for all children.

Child Development

Beat Plastics Pollution

Raising awareness about harmful plastic waste on ecosystems and human health. Encouraging individuals and businesses to take collective action to protect the planet.

Environment

Skilling A Youth

Creating positivity, resilience, determination, and spiritual nurture mainly for the girl child and woman, empowering them to take control of their social and economic futures.

Empowerment

Reusable Sanitary Pad Making

Providing training and resources to Bondo Village, empowering community members to take control of their menstrual health while generating income.

Health & Income

Nutrition for Adolescent Mothers

Focused nutrition programs in Terego District, West Nile Region, to support young mothers and their children’s health and development.

Nutrition

Climate Change Outreach

Mentoring adolescent women and young girls in Buliisa on environmental sustainability. (Launched December 17, 2023).

Climate Action
Action

Ending Corporal Punishment

Turning Pledge into Action: Working towards ending corporal punishment globally to ensure a safe and humane environment for every child.

Child Safety
Literacy

Multilingual Education

Promoting multilingual education and literacy for cohesion and social-economic transformation across our communities.
