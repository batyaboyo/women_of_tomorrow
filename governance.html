Our Governance

Accountability & Transparency

At WOTDO, we believe that effective community transformation requires the highest standards of governance. Our operations are guided by a commitment to ethical leadership, financial transparency, and accountability to the communities we serve.

Our Board of Directors brings together experts from education, public health, law, and business. They provide strategic oversight, ensuring that our resources are maximized and that every program aligns strictly with our mission and the UN SDGs.

Annual Audits

WOTDO conducts independent financial audits annually, ensuring that donor contributions strictly fund designated rural and youth empowerment programs.
